QueryPic Privacy Policy

Effective date: Thursday 8th October, 2026.
Contact: querypicsupport@gmail.com.

1. Core promise

QueryPic searches your photos and videos entirely on your device. Your media, embeddings, OCR text, transcripts, and search queries never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

2. What stays on-device (always)

Media files and OS metadata we read (via folder access you grant); the search index (SQLite + vectors) in the app sandbox; settings and license key. We operate no servers that receive or store your media.

3. Optional diagnostics (default OFF, your choice)

Two separate opt-ins, both off until you turn them on in Settings or onboarding:

  • Anonymous usage counts (provider: Aptabase, EU-hosted): event names only, e.g. "paywall viewed" — never query text, paths, or vectors. Includes coarse country derived from the connection (e.g. Nigeria) and OS/app version.
  • Crash reports (provider: GlitchTip): scrubbed stack trace, OS version, device model. File paths, queries, GPS, and account identity are stripped on-device before sending. Note: GlitchTip's managed hosting currently logs the sender connection IP alongside the report.

Turn either off at any time; the app works fully offline with both off (Airplane Mode included).

4. Permissions we request

Media/video read (only folders you pick); Internet (only for the two opt-in diagnostics above and opening the Paystack checkout page in your browser). No location, contacts, or microphone permissions.

5. Payments

Pro purchases go through Paystack's website in your browser under Paystack's own privacy terms — card details never touch QueryPic. After payment you receive a license key by email, entered in Settings to unlock Pro.

6. Children

No accounts, no user profiles, nothing knowingly collected from children beyond the above.

7. Changes

Material changes will be noted in-app before they take effect.
